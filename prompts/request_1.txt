I want to build a **Wind Chime simulation app** using **Flutter + Flame**.

The goal is to create a physically interactive wind-chime experience where the chime's movement and sound are influenced by both **real-world weather conditions** and **the user's physical interaction with the phone**.

## Core Concept

The app displays a **2D wind chime simulation**.

The wind chime consists of:

* A rope/string from which the chime hangs
* Multiple vertical metal rods/tubes
* A central hanging ball/clapper
* Optional sail at the bottom
* Simple physics-based movement
* Collision detection between the ball and rods
* Sound generated whenever the ball hits a rod

The simulation should feel natural rather than behaving like a simple animation.

# 3. Flame Physics Simulation

The Flame engine should be responsible for the actual 2D simulation.

The simulation receives:

```text
windSpeed
windDirection
deviceTilt
deviceShake
```

and calculates:

* Wind force
* Chime movement
* Ball/clapper movement
* Rod movement
* Collisions
* Collision intensity
* Sound events

The wind chime should behave like a physically plausible system.

For example:

```text
Wind Speed
    ↓
Wind Force
    ↓
Chime/Sail movement
    ↓
Rope + Ball dynamics
    ↓
Ball trajectory
    ↓
Collision with rod
    ↓
Collision intensity
    ↓
Sound
```

I don't necessarily need perfect real-world physics, but the simulation should **feel believable and responsive**.

Please recommend whether we should:

* Build our own lightweight physics simulation
* Use Flame's built-in components/physics capabilities
* Use another Flutter/Flame-compatible physics approach
