What's new

Sample bank synthesized from the tube physics. There are no recordings, so every tube gets 8 samples generated at startup, 40 in all, in about 0.2 s:

2 strike hardnesses. A harder, shorter strike reaches higher partials, so it sounds brighter.
2 strike positions. A hit at the middle of a tube can't sound its second partial, so moving the strike point changes the timbre.
2 takes for alternation.
The layout is data, so real recordings can replace the synthesis later without other changes. Decoded, the bank uses about 26 MB of memory.

Choosing each sound: loudness follows hit strength. The hard layer blends in gradually with random overlap, so the switch is never audible; glancing blows sound duller. A tube never plays the same sample twice in a row.

Voice management:

at most 3 voices per tube and 24 overall;
old voices always fade out rather than being cut, so no clicks;
soft hits are skipped once there are more than 10 hits per second;
a clapper resting on a ringing tube muffles it.
Wind ambience follows the live wind, so you hear a gust just before the chime answers it.

Mix: a global reverb, then a limiter.

Audio session and app lifecycle:

on iOS the ringer switch won't silence the chime, and other apps' music can play alongside;
on Android it plays as media without taking audio focus;
phone calls and leaving the app fade the sound out and pause the audio hardware; returning fades it back in.
Two physics problems the audio work exposed, now fixed

Muffling fired far too often. On the first run, 79% of breeze notes got damped. The contact solver refused to bounce anything slower than 4 cm/s, and a breeze's taps land at 2–6 cm/s, so the clapper stuck to the tube. With a 1 cm/s threshold, damping drops to 22% at 3 m/s, and 43% at 6 m/s, where steady wind really does lean the clapper on the downwind tubes. Damping also now requires real contact, not just hovering within 1 mm.
A string could stretch in a jam. The 10-minute stress test caught a tube string stretched 12% when the clapper jammed at the tube tops. A second constraint pass after contacts fixed it.
